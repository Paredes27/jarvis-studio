Side Quest Radar

Side Quest Radar makes short, original videos about new and under-the-radar PC games, and publishes them to social networks. Every video is reviewed before it goes out, and every post goes to an account that its owner has connected themselves.

What we do

  • Discover. We follow new game releases and announcements and pick the ones worth a short video.
  • Make. We produce short vertical videos (about 20 to 60 seconds) with our own narration, captions and editing, using the games' official trailers and images.
  • Publish. Our web app, Side Quest Radar Publisher, schedules and publishes the finished videos to the TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts that the account owner has connected through each platform's official sign-in.

How the TikTok connection works

The owner of a TikTok account signs in through TikTok's official authorization screen and approves four permissions. The app only reads the account's basic profile (name and username) and sends the videos the owner chose to publish. Before each post the owner sees which account will receive it, chooses who can view it and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, declares any commercial content, previews the video and confirms. Nothing is sent to TikTok before that confirmation.

The owner can disconnect the account at any time, inside the app and in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. See the Privacy Policy for details on the data we handle and the Terms of Service for the rules of use.
