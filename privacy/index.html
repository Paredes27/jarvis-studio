Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Nexus Publisher, the internal tool behind Jarvis Studio, uses, and why.

1. Who uses the tool

Only the owner of Jarvis Studio uses Nexus Publisher, and only for our own accounts (GamingJarvis and JarvisTests). It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not collect data from viewers or other users.

2. Data we access

We access the following, with our own authorization, through the official APIs of TikTok, Instagram and YouTube:

  • basic profile information of our own accounts (account ID, username);
  • statistics of our own accounts and videos (followers, views, likes, comments, shares);
  • the access tokens needed to post our videos and read those statistics.

We never access other people's accounts, messages or personal data.

3. How we use and store it

  • The statistics are used only to measure how our own videos perform.
  • Tokens travel only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and are kept on our private server in a file that only the tool can read. They are never shared or written to logs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the accounts are active. To stop all access, we revoke the tool in each platform's settings and delete the stored tokens and statistics from our server.

5. Google and YouTube

For YouTube, Nexus Publisher uses the YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke an app's access to a Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in our repository or send a direct message to one of our accounts.
