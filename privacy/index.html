2. What the Service is

Side Quest Radar Publisher publishes original short videos to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts that their owners have connected through each platform's official authorization screen. The website itself does not use accounts, advertising or analytics cookies.

3. Data we receive from TikTok

When an account owner connects TikTok, we request four permissions (scopes) and nothing else:

ScopeWhat we receive or doWhy
user.info.basicThe account's open ID and display name; creator info (nickname, privacy options available, interaction settings, maximum video length).To identify the connected account and to show the owner which account will receive a post and which options TikTok allows.
user.info.profileThe account's username (the @name).To show "Connected as @username" in the app.
video.publishPermission to publish a video the owner has approved, with the settings the owner chose.To post to the owner's profile after the owner confirms.
video.uploadPermission to upload a video as a draft to the owner's TikTok inbox.For accounts where direct posting is not available: the owner finishes the post inside TikTok.

We do not read the account's followers, messages, contacts, viewing history or other people's data, and we do not receive the account's password.

4. Other data we handle

  • Videos, titles and captions that the owner chooses to publish, and the publishing settings chosen for each post.
  • Technical records of what was published and when (post status, time, error messages), kept to run the Service and to resolve problems.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, Instagram and YouTube so that the app can publish on the owner's behalf.

The Service is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

5. How we store and protect it

Access tokens are stored in a file with restricted permissions (readable only by the service user) on the server run by the operator. They are sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS), never written to logs, and never shown in the app. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We share data only with the platform you publish to (to carry out your post) and with infrastructure providers needed to run the server.

6. How long we keep it

  • Tokens are kept while the account is connected and deleted when you disconnect it.
  • Publishing records are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.
  • Videos are kept only until they have been published, or until you delete them.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect. In the app, open Connect account and press Disconnect on the TikTok row. This deletes the stored tokens on our server; it does not revoke the authorization on TikTok's side. To revoke it too, go to TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites.
  • Your rights. Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD). Write to [PREENCHER: email da SQR] and we will answer within one month.

8. Third-party platforms

When you connect an account, the platform's own terms and privacy policy also apply: TikTok Privacy Policy, Instagram Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. For YouTube, the Service uses the YouTube API Services, and you can revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
